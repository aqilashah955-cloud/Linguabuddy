Ayesha: Today I want to convince you of one small habit that can change your English completely. Read for twenty minutes every day. Here are my three reasons. First, reading builds vocabulary faster than memorizing word lists. When you meet a new word inside a story, your brain connects it to the characters and events, so you remember it far longer than a word copied ten times in a notebook. Second, reading quietly trains your grammar. You might not notice it, but as you read correct sentences again and again, your own writing starts to follow the same patterns. Your sentences become longer, clearer, and more correct without extra effort. Third, and most important, reading gives you ideas. Students who read have more to say in discussions, more examples in essays, and more confidence when they speak. So here is my challenge. Twenty minutes. One story. Every day. Try it for one month, and watch what happens to your English.
