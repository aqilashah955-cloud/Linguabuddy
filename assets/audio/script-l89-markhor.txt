Narrator: The markhor is a wild mountain goat found in the high mountains of Pakistan. It is easy to recognize because of its long, twisted horns, which spiral upward like a corkscrew. Male markhors grow a long beard and thick fur to survive the freezing winters. These strong animals climb steep cliffs with amazing balance, jumping from rock to rock where no human could follow. The markhor is Pakistan's national animal, a symbol of courage and strength. Sadly, its numbers fell in the past because of hunting and the loss of its forest home. Today, local communities protect the markhor, and its population is slowly growing again. Protecting the markhor also protects the mountains, the forests, and the clean water that villages below depend on.
